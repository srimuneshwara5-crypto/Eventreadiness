    Questions

    Where is my data saved?

    In this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded. Clearing browser data may remove your saved events.

    Can I add my own tasks?

    Yes. Every category has an Add task box, and every task can be deleted.

    Can I print a checklist?

    Use your browser's print option. The tracker is formatted for printing.

    Does it work on my phone?

    Yes. The layout adapts to smaller screens.
